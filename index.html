Staravorix

A world written for over a decade

A universe of my own. Burning gods, drifted stars, and long campaigns that outlive their characters. The maps get redrawn every time someone survives something they should not have.

Runes are markers in reality that tell reality to pay attention. The better they are drawn, the more eye-catching they are to the world.

Vintage Story

Mods for a quiet, stubborn world

Some worlds reward patience. I build mods for one of them: slow systems that make a long life feel earned.

  • Spreading Devastationthe Rot creeps outward, and machines built from copper, gears, and flux push it back
  • Temporal Storms Require a Fightstorms you survive by standing your ground
  • Seraph Levelingtrait progression for long lives
  • Underwater Horrorssea monsters under calm water
  • Nadiya Village Assistancevillages that grow when you are generous to them

They come together in Rustfall, a modpack that starts you on a small island in a devastated world, with one rift ward holding the dark back.

AI Alignment Research

I like opening the box

When an agent’s context gets summarized, do your preferences survive? I build evals that answer small questions like that exactly, because labs steer by what gets measured.

PrefDecay-Eval · Apart Research · 2026
95%before compaction
80%after one summary
72%after six

Make the preference a tool parameter and adherence becomes an exact check, no LLM judge. The summarizer turned out to be the culprit: routing compaction to a stronger model took the weakest cell from 23% to 92%.

It started with an Apart hackathon win: a Minecraft agent that drifted off its goal, and the safety layer that caught it.

Convergence

My philosophy

All things have an end. A thrown stone cannot change its path; a stalled plane can wake its engine and choose another. Convergence is the practice of finding the ends worth reaching, and steering toward them.

Destinations are determined by the steps we take.
  1. The Starknow what you want
  2. The Constellationmap the cause and effect
  3. The Hierarchycompress it into principles
  4. The Waydraw a plan
  5. The Stepact
  6. The Eyelook back and refine
  7. The Cyclebegin again, a little closer

Things I’ve Made

The long thread

What is the most beautiful thing you have made so far? Here is my answer, in order. Each one was practice for the next.

  1. Age 5
    Writing

    A story told out loud

    Every night, a story for my little brother. Magic powers, and nobody ever dies. It never really stopped.

  2. Middle school
    Writing

    The first 120 pages

    The world that would become Staravorix, written until Word crashed and I learned to save everything.

  3. 2019
    VR game

    City Breaker

    My first published game: break a city apart in virtual reality. Built in high school, shipped in my first year of college.

  4. 2019
    Nonprofit

    CommunityVR

    Bringing VR headsets to nursing-home residents in Kane County, for a little wonder in their day.

  5. 2020
    Writing

    Leap Motion

    Technical writer on a hand-tracking handbook, working with David Holz.

  6. 2020
    VR game

    Hanger VR

    A grappling-gun game across a constellation map. The first thing I made that found fans.

  7. 2021
    Game

    FitFight

    Push-ups become stats. A combat game you level up by getting stronger in real life.

  8. 2022
    Art

    Dreaming in a Warped World

    A webcomic made in the earliest days of AI art, as one of Midjourney’s first few hundred users.

  9. 2023
    Tool

    fast-email.ai

    An AI email writer that grew into a set of business tools. Graduated from Grinnell in computer science, with a neuroscience concentration.

  10. 2024
    Research

    The Minecraft god-agent

    An agent that drifted off its goal and the safety layer that caught it. It won an Apart Research hackathon, and led to the lab. Also that year: the RimWorld Infinite Implants mod.

  11. 2025
    Game

    Civilization Sim

    One of the best things I’ve built. I learned more about history and technology from it than from anything else. Also: Life Leveling, FitFight grown up.

  12. 2026
    Now

    Vintage Story mods, and PrefDecay-Eval

    A flurry of mods, a published eval on preference decay in agents, and a talk called Making Beautiful Things.
